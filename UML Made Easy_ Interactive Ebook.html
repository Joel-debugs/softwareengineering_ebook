UML Made Easy

Draw your software before you build it

The Unified Modeling Language (UML) is a standard visual language for describing software systems[1]. It was created in the 1990s by Booch, Rumbaugh and Jacobson and is now maintained by the Object Management Group[1][2]. You don’t need all of it: Fowler notes that a small core of diagrams covers most real-world needs[3].
